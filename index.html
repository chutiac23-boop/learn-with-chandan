WE'RE HERE TO HELP

Help & support

Find guidance for using the platform and getting help with common issues.

How do I find a lesson?

Choose your class, then subject, chapter and lesson. You can also use the search bar.

Can I download study notes?

PDF notes and selected study materials will be available for download when published.

Do I need an account to visit?

No. Students can browse free learning content without signing in. An account will be needed for cloud-saved progress and personal features.

Where can I report a problem?

Use the Contact section once its contact details have been activated by the site administrator.
